# Data availability

The software repository does not redistribute raw sequence reads or the full
third-party rice dataset.

Raw *Vaccinium* amplicon reads are available from ENA or NCBI under
PRJEB110492, PRJEB35843, PRJEB98254, PRJNA1156347, PRJNA835240,
PRJNA1253324, PRJNA577971, and PRJNA578171. The field-rice source data are
available from Dryad at <https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.7q7k1>. The Arabidopsis
demonstration subset is distributed with CoreMicrobiomeR 0.1.0.

Harmonized metadata, analysis-ready tables, taxonomic crosswalks,
sample-exclusion records, figure source data, and exact result tables are
prepared as a separate companion dataset. Its Zenodo DOI will be recorded in
the mSystems article after the author publishes the data record.
